## GRI 2: General Disclosures — company profile

Nordwind Energy S.p.A. is an Italian energy company headquartered in Milan, Italy. The legal form of the company is a joint-stock company (Società per Azioni). The reporting period covered by this sustainability report is the financial year 2023, running from 1 January 2023 to 31 December 2023. The report was published in 2024. At the end of the reporting period the company employed 12,480 people in total, of whom 5,616 were women, corresponding to 45% of the workforce. The highest governance body is the Board of Directors, which has eight members and three committees: the Audit Committee, the Remuneration Committee and the Sustainability Committee. Three of the eight board members are non-executive independent directors.

## GRI 302: Energy consumption

During the 2023 reporting year Nordwind Energy consumed 320,000 MWh of electricity in its own operations and facilities. Renewable electricity accounted for 78% of total electricity consumption, mainly from wind and photovoltaic sources. Heat consumption amounted to 42,500 GJ, while steam consumption was 18,200 GJ and cooling consumption was 9,400 GJ. The company continued to replace gas-fired boilers with electric heat pumps at its Milan and Turin sites. All consumption figures refer to the operational boundary defined in the 2022 report and have not been restated.

## GRI 305: Emissions — Scope 1, Scope 2 and Scope 3

Gross direct (Scope 1) GHG emissions for the 2023 reporting year were 84,320 tCO2e. Scope 2 indirect emissions amounted to 51,200 tCO2e on a location-based basis and 12,900 tCO2e on a market-based basis, reflecting the company's renewable electricity procurement. Gross indirect (Scope 3) GHG emissions were 215,600 tCO2e, with the largest contributions from purchased goods and services and from use of sold products. Emissions have been calculated following the GHG Protocol Corporate Standard; emission factors were taken from the DEFRA 2023 conversion factors and from the IEA 2023 electricity database. The base year for the emissions inventory is 2019.

## GRI 401: Employment — hires and turnover

In 2023 Nordwind hired 1,142 new employees, of whom 512 were women. Total employee turnover was 8.4%, calculated as the number of departures divided by the average number of employees during the year. The largest share of hires occurred in the engineering and operations functions. Employee figures are reported for consolidated operations in Italy, Germany and France.

## GRI 403: Occupational health and safety

There were zero work-related fatalities among employees and contractors in the 2023 reporting year. The number and rate of high-consequence work-related injuries, excluding fatalities, was 3, corresponding to a rate of 0.24 per 100 full-time employees. Employees worked a total of 24,960,000 hours during the year. All sites carried out annual risk assessments and near-miss reporting procedures were rolled out to all operational facilities.
